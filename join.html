Join us

Join AADL

AADL welcomes graduate and undergraduate students interested in aircraft conceptual design, electric propulsion, mission-level operation analysis, and AI-based design.

Who we are looking for

We are looking for researchers who want to design the aircraft of tomorrow.

Contact the professor by e-mail with a short CV and your interests.

항공기 개념설계, 전기추진, AI 기반 설계에 관심 있는 대학원생과 학부연구생을 상시 모집합니다.

Research directions students can join

01

Simulation-Based System Design

  • Development of the RISPECT+ conceptual design tool
  • Physics-based electric propulsion system modeling

Read more about this research area

02

Simulation-Based System Operation

  • Mission-oriented measures of effectiveness
  • Optimal deployment and strategies for missions

Read more about this research area

03

Experiment-Based System Analysis

  • Battery and motor–propeller testing
  • Physics-based analysis models with Gaussian process regression

Read more about this research area

Eligibility

Who can apply

대상 : 한서대학교 재학생 또는 휴학생 / 학부연구생 활동을 지속할 수 있는 학생 (우대사항: 성적우수자, 군필자) / 3, 4학년의 경우, 대학원 진학을 희망하는 학생

Open to students currently enrolled at, or on leave from, Hanseo University who can continue undergraduate-research activity (preference for a strong academic record and completed military service); third- and fourth-year students who wish to proceed to graduate school.

Graduate program

The lab's graduate program is the M.S. in Aeronautical System Engineering, Hanseo University.

How to apply

E-mail Prof. Donguk Lee (dulee@hanseo.ac.kr) with a short CV and your interests.
