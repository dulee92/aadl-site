Faculty

Principal Investigator

Donguk Lee 이동욱, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Department of Unmanned Aircraft Systems, Hanseo University (Mar. 2023 – present)

His research interests are the conceptual design of UAM/AAM and UAV systems, electric and hybrid-electric propulsion system modeling, mission-oriented system-of-systems operation, experiment-based battery and motor–propeller analysis, and AI-assisted and digital-engineering design workflows.

Education
  • Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering, Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Seoul National University, Mar. 2019 – Feb. 2023. Dissertation: "Generic System Design Methodology for eVTOL Aircraft using Enhanced Electric Propulsion Modeling"
  • M.S. in Aerospace Engineering, Seoul National University, Mar. 2017 – Feb. 2019
  • B.S. in Aerospace Engineering, Korea Aerospace University, Mar. 2011 – Feb. 2017
Positions
  • Deputy Director, Convergence and Open Sharing System (COSS) project in advanced fields (aviation and drones), Hanseo University 첨단분야 혁신융합대학사업(항공드론) 부단장
  • Director, Aerospace Drone Center, Glocal University 30 project, Hanseo University 글로컬대학 항공우주드론센터 센터장
